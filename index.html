02 — Model analysis

How the estimator behaves

Three models were trained on the KIBA benchmark and scored on two protocols. The primary metric is the Concordance Index (CI), which measures ranking quality.

Leaderboard — Concordance Index

6 RUNS

Random split rewards memorising known drugs; the cold-drug split holds out entire molecules and is the realistic estimate.

Learning curve — cold-drug CI

XGBOOST

Test CI rises steadily as training drugs increase (50 → 1,600), so data diversity — not model capacity — is the limiting factor.
